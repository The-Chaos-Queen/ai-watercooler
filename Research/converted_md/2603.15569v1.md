5.4 TheStateSpaceModelViewpoint
Althoughmodernrecurrentmodelshaveseveraldifferentviewpointsthatlargelyconverge(Section5.1),eachframework

WeintroduceMamba-3,astatespacemodelwithseveralmethodologicalimprovementsoverpriorSSMs:amorepower-
