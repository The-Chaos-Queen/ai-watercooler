## Risks / Watch Out For
- **Bnb 4-bit loading:** Gemma-4 is incompatible with quantized 4-bit loads via standard transformers conversions without throwing weight dimension mismatch errors. We must maintain `bf16` precision for Gemma inference runs.
- **Pending envelope gate:** The saved G0 vectors are marked with `envelope_status: "unvalidated_pending_DQ1a"` in their metadata. Downstream injection must remain blocked until the MED envelope re-unit (DQ1a) is completed.
