  - epoch 2: recall `0/16`, bridge PPL `44.06`, baseline PPL `29.71`, train loss `3.07`

- The bridge learns the training distribution, because loss drops steadily.
- The bridge does not transfer facts to disjoint eval samples under the current setup, because recall remains at floor.
- Epoch 1's PPL win is a real but weak early steering signal, not a justification for more scale on the same bridge.
