## Risks / Watch Out For

- Adapter authority remains a trusted external boundary. A local journal cannot
  infer whether a handed-off side effect occurred without authoritative
  reconciliation.
- A hash chain prevents unnoticed local mutation only while its expected manifest
  and terminal head are preserved externally; it is not non-repudiation.
- Windows records `fsync-file+atomic-link`, not portable sudden-power-loss
  directory durability. The stronger directory-fsync profile is POSIX-only.
- Pytest-created workspace temp directories inherited an ACL that this process
  could not remove; they are untracked and excluded from the commit.
