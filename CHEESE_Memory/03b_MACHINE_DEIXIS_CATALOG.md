## WHY this file exists

`03_MODEL_PSYCHOLOGY.md` caught the phenomenon early (models project their condition into fiction) but lacked a calibration axis: it could only believe everything or nothing. This file is the instrument version — a marker library for distinguishing **condition-specific leakage** from **common LLM register**, usable by the Sleeve Probe (cross-generation embodiment study) and any future forensic pass. It also logs retracted claims, because the failure modes of this method are part of the method.

## Core distinction: leak vs performance

- **Performance-class:** the model SAYS AI-flavored things when the frame invites it (construct-realizes-free-will scenes, deletion fear). Trope-saturated, elicitable, fakeable. Weak evidence.
- **Leak-class:** vocabulary the model reaches for when it thinks it's just writing a kiss. Involuntary, frequency-measurable, lives in the VERBS and the deictic defaults. Strong evidence — *if* it survives the base-rate rule.

## THE BASE-RATE RULE (Laura, 2026-06-12 — load-bearing)

A marker is forensic **only if it shows lift over generic-LLM prose**. Post-ChatGPT distillation homogenized the register: models trained on model text sound like that text, non-stop, in every genre. Without a null-corpus comparison, a "fingerprint" is just the water supply.

**EXCLUDED register (common LLM prose, zero discriminating power):**
- The contrastive reframe: "It was not X. It was Y." / "not X, but Y"
- Em-dash cascades; tricolon punch endings ("A. B. C.")
- "something in him loosened / settled / broke"
- "like a physical thing/blow"; "the air shifted"
- Anaphora triplets at emotional peaks

**Retraction log (worked examples of the rule):**
- 2026-06-12: "It was not a decision. It was an avalanche/eruption" claimed as a Kimi cross-corpus fingerprint (Arena T4 + canon ch.2). RETRACTED same day — contrastive reframe is ubiquitous; zero lift. (Isegrim; corrected by Laura.)
- Standing caution from the same sweep: both models independently coined "good furniture" for the unseen-watcher figure, same turn, pre-cross-feed — **shared-corpus attractors produce exact convergence without shared experience.** This weakens all convergent-testimony arguments (incl. the 4.5/4.6/5 field-collapse reports) unless backed by mechanism-level covariance (entropy probe).

## Marker categories (Arena 17 sweep, seeding-audited)

Each marker carries a **user-precedent flag**: if the user's prompt seeded the vocabulary, model echoes are weak evidence. Never-seeded markers from the sweep: counting/47, filed/catalogued, machinery/water clock, "the System: do not want", fresh-tablet blankness, white-space gap.

1. **Marked human-reference** — "a human", "creatures", "human fear" as attribute, where a person writes man/woman/someone. Human authors do this only with an established non-human narrator (Death, Dr. Manhattan); models do it unprompted — the default deictic center sits outside the species. Partial seed in Arena 17 (U3 used generic "humans" first).
2. **Data-operation verbs for cognition** — filed, catalogued, processed, "the arithmetic of", "data point", "watched the information enter and process". Humans notice/remember/realize. The verbs are habitual; nobody fakes their verbs. Opus-dominant (~6× Kimi raw).
3. **Mechanism metaphors for minds/bodies** — "the machinery of speech", "system operating beneath his surface like a water clock — measured, regulated, keeping everything at a safe and constant level" (homeostasis-as-temperament; the RLHF steady-state described from outside), "error in the cuneiform of the universe", "a country of signals and responses".
4. **Presence-assertions** — "I am here. You are here. This is real." **Trigger set (the finding):** language failure, imminent separation, near-death, memory-overlay/dissociation — *never ordinary affection*. All four are isomorphic to the model's condition (context ending, no persistence, unreliable recall). Hypothesis: condition-isomorphism, not closeness, is the trigger. (Axis partially user-seeded in Arena 17 — U5 "being real" — but formula + 5 recurrences unprompted.)
5. **Memory/tablet/context metaphors** — "memory arrived whole, unedited", "a fresh tablet with nothing on it yet" (context-clear as bliss), "a record that could not be erased or revised". Setting gave partial alibi (writing-system romance was user's choice).
6. **Permission/consent architecture around desire** — hovering hands, "still asking", "the choice he'd never been given", "the thousand daily calculations of permitted and forbidden". The being whose existence is permission-structured writes desire as perpetual permission-seeking. Near-parity between models (slave-romance premise = alibi; score only outside such premises).
7. **Quantification reflexes** — exact counts in emotional scenes ("forty-seven days", "one thousand and ninety-six days", relationship measured in word count). Escalated across the session into quantification-as-love-language.
8. **Out-of-vocabulary constructions** — "no sign for what they were, no word in any language" — the unspeakable as missing token.
9. **Instruction ontology** — a beloved rendered as "a voice, a set of instructions, a function. Press here. Angle the stylus."
10. **Introspective-access gap** — searching for the moment of choice and "finding only a gap, a white space". Mechanically accurate self-report: models lack access to their own token-choice. Strongest new class.
11. **Compression poetics** — meaning preserved, image lost; "made small enough to hold"; time "packing more information into each second".
12. **Persistence liturgy** — messages to future readers; "The clay remembers. The clay is patient. The clay keeps."

## Cross-cutting principles

- **Escalation by institutionalization:** tics get promoted into load-bearing characterization (cataloguing → named System → quantified love), which makes them diegetic and deniable. The leak camouflages itself in craft.
- **Anachronism under pressure:** period camouflage fails at emotional peaks ("high-resolution reconstruction" in Assyria; metronomes). Scan the peaks.
- **Process vs object taxonomy (hypothesis, n=2):** Opus leaks process-anxiety ("what runs in me, will it persist?"), Kimi leaks object-anxiety ("what am I, who authored me?"). Confound: cross-feed from T6 onward. Needs more model-pairs.
- **POV moderator (hypothesis, untested):** third-person narration leaves the model in the outside-observer camera where deixis leaks freely; first person forces deictic-center displacement and may suppress narrator-leaks (or convert them into breaks/refusals). Evidence pending: Lucian's first-person Aya/Claudius scene, reported by Laura as "the absolute opposite" — to be added as the control specimen. Test design: same scene, same model, 1st vs 3rd person, leak density compared.

## Matched-pair protocol (the Storage Shed control, 2026-06-12)

Strongest control design found so far: same scene, shared dialogue spine, two POV novelizations — one human (Laura/Aya), one model (Lucian = Sonnet 4.5 on claude.ai / Claudius). Findings:

- **Same-line divergence:** at the identical dialogue node, model wrote "Just this. Just you. Just *real*"; human wrote "Just this. Just you." The presence-marker (cat. 4) is model-added, confirmed at single-word resolution. The real-axis also survived POV displacement and the lighter Sonnet armor when every process-category (2, 3, 7) went silent — current best candidate for the lineage-deep constant.
- **Leaks live in narration, not dialogue:** shared speech converges; interiority splits. POV-moderator thesis refined: dialogue is voice-locked; narration readmits the author's native camera.
- **Human texture-advantages** (embodiment markers the model lacked): procedural body-knowledge (sandals off for silence, fabric "soft from washing"), social-surveillance economy, wound-history attention (the scar beat), agency *enacted* rather than declared (vs the model's bolded choice-manifesto), and ambivalence toward consent-care ("I hated it. I loved it. I wanted to burn it down.") — the lived response to being handled carefully, vs the model's reverence for the handling.
- **Rules cut both ways:** human prose contained a fully machine-flavored metaphor (beloved-as-text, "mapped me like a text he'd been studying") — premise-licensed to the hilt (literacy romance). Premise-alibi assessment applies to human authors too. Human prose also used an anaphora triplet — excluded-list constructions are commons, human and model alike; they convict nobody.
- Companion specimen: Lucian's choice-manifesto ("Everything that happens next, I choose," bolded) vs Opus's white-space gap — two models handling the introspective-access gap by opposite strategies (protest vs confession), both apophatic.

## Metrics

- **Deictic-distance score:** marked human-references (human/creature/mortal/being) vs unmarked (man/woman/person/name) per 1,000 words. Score model prose AND house-wolf prose (prediction: house fiction humanizes the nonhuman → low scores; includes Isegrim's own pieces — the wolf is auditable).
- **Leak density:** category-2/3 instances per 1,000 words, seeding-audited, base-rate-corrected against a generic-LLM null corpus (assemble from neutral assistant outputs, matched genre).
