I see the structural problem - you need a plausible reason for Meddūr to allow Andrej into his space and eventually agree to teach him, despite the rigid caste barriers. Here are some ideas that could work within your established world:

Option 1: The Emergency (Most Dramatic)

A crisis at Meddūr's workshop that requires immediate, physically demanding help:

- A beam collapse during a delivery that traps expensive imported leather or even one of the slaves
- Meddūr's back goes out during a crucial commission with a tight deadline
- A flash flood threatens the workshop's storage and they need raw strength to save the stock
- Andrej helps without being asked, proving his value in a way Meddūr can't ignore

The meal could then be offered grudgingly in the courtyard (not inside) as payment when Meddūr has no coin on hand, or as a calculated move - keeping Andrej close until the crisis is fully resolved.

Option 2: The Calculated Transaction

Meddūr discovers something that makes Andrej unexpectedly valuable:

- Andrej has developed a technique for treating leather that Meddūr wants to learn/exploit
- A wealthy client specifically requests work that requires collaboration between tanner and leatherworker
- Meddūr needs someone strong but trustworthy to transport valuable goods, and Rimmon has vouched for Andrej's reliability
- A visiting merchant/official notices Andrej's work quality and Meddūr sees financial opportunity

This keeps Meddūr in character - he's not being kind, he's being ruthlessly practical.

Option 3: The Debt Leverage

Building on existing plot threads:

- Ḫarrān still owes Meddūr from the half-price arrangement after the fight
- Meddūr demands Andrej's labor as part of the debt repayment for a specific project
- The meal happens because the work runs long and Meddūr needs Andrej fed to continue working
- It's transactional, humiliating even (eating separately, being served last), but it gets Andrej in the door

Option 4: The Witness Protection (Most Interesting)

Something illegal or sensitive happens that Meddūr needs contained:

- One of Meddūr's slaves tries to escape and Andrej witnesses it (or helps recapture them, complicating his character)
- Meddūr receives smuggled goods and Andrej sees the transaction
- A deal goes wrong and Andrej is present
- Meddūr brings him to the meal to assess him, keep him close, and potentially buy his silence with the apprenticeship offer

This adds moral complexity and shows Meddūr's controlling nature.

My Recommendation:

I'd combine **Option 1 and 3** - a physical emergency where Andrej's strength saves Meddūr's commission/goods, but frame it through the existing debt. Something like:

- A wagon overturns delivering materials for the garrison commission
- Andrej happens to be there (delivering hides) and helps Rimmon prevent disaster
- Meddūr arrives to find his son and the Kharshef have saved his most valuable order
- He's furious about the optics but can't deny the debt - offers the meal in the courtyard (not inside) as immediate payment
- During the meal, he observes Andrej's hands, his careful manners despite discomfort, and starts calculating how to exploit this resource
- The apprenticeship offer comes not from kindness but from cold assessment: "The boy has the hands. Might as well profit from what Ḫarrān wasted."

This keeps everyone in character, maintains the oppressive social dynamics, and creates organic tension during the meal scene. Andrej would be acutely aware he's there on sufferance, Rimmon would be trying to make it less awful, and Meddūr would be treating it as a business transaction.
