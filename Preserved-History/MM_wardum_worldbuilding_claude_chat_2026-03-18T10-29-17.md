## Claude

This is a strong opening that establishes several things efficiently:

**What's working well:**

- The "growth spurts" as physical displacement perfectly mirrors Andrej's perpetual sense of not belonging
- Rimmon noticing the changes Andrej can't/won't see himself

- The headwrap description grounds us in the practical realities of Andrej's existence
- "Any hour spent as 'useful hands' for Rimmon was an hour he wasn't a ghost" is a perfect distillation of what Rimmon means to him

**Potential areas to consider:**

1. **The two-week absence feels unexplained.** Rimmon says "you haven't been around," but Andrej's deflection ("Ḫarrān needed me") feels thin. Two weeks is a long time for them not to have crossed paths, especially given their established pattern. You might need:
- A more substantial reason (was there increased work? A specific commission? Did Andrej deliberately avoid the workshop?)
- Or acknowledgment that Andrej *chose* not to go, even though he could have
2. **Rimmon's energy seems unchanged.** Given the weight of what just happened with Ellin (the arrangement, the performance, the dirt on his knee), Rimmon feels almost too light here—"all quickness and blue cloth." Consider:
- Is he performing normalcy?
- Is he *relieved* to be with Andrej after two weeks of playing roles?
- Does something in his manner feel off to Andrej, even if he can't name it?
3. **The roof repair feels convenient** rather than organic. If the roof has been "coming loose," why now? Why not a week ago? Consider:
- Making it *Rimmon's* urgency rather than his father's ("I can't stand another day in that workshop")
- Or having it be genuine crisis ("the wind last night finally tore it")
- Or dropping the pretense entirely—Rimmon just shows up and says "come with me"
4. **Pacing consideration:** You mention potentially slowing the chapter down. This opening moves efficiently but could breathe more if you wanted to linger on:
- Andrej's physical self-consciousness in more detail
- The walk through the village (what *specific* obstacles? who looks away?)
- The moment Rimmon appears—does Andrej's body react before he consciously registers his friend's presence?

**A structural question:**
What's the *inciting incident* of this chapter? The roof repair feels like setup, not the actual story. Is something going to happen at the shed? Does someone follow them? Do they have a conversation that shifts something? Knowing what you're building toward might help you calibrate how much to slow/expand this opening.

The opening is solid—it just might need some threading to connect it more organically to what came before and what's coming next. What were the specific setup issues you mentioned that aren't working the way you thought?
