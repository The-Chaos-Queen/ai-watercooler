Consider what just happened:

- Pontodoros planted doubt

- Rimmon *immediately* reverted to the pattern: create chaos, force a reaction, weaponize his brokenness

1. Andrej to articulate what he needs (boundary-setting, building vs. surviving)
2. Rimmon to accept responsibility without deflecting into performance

4. Both to have the tools to actually change

**At 16-ish and fresh from trauma (fleeing home, survival mode across the flats)?** They might *want* to be this mature. They might even *mean* it in the moment. But do they have the capacity to sustain it?
