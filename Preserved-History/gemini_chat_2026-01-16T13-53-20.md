## Gemini

Almond drink (commonly called almond milk) is generally considered **healthy** for most people, but its "healthiness" depends heavily on which version you buy and what you're using it to replace.

It is naturally low in calories and high in Vitamin E, but it can become "unhealthy" if it’s loaded with added sugars or if you rely on it as a primary protein source.

The "Healthy" Side- **Heart Health:** It is naturally cholesterol-free and low in saturated fat. It contains heart-healthy monounsaturated fats.+1
- **Low Calorie:** Unsweetened almond milk typically has only **30–50 calories** per cup, making it great for weight management.
- **Vitamin E:** It is an excellent source of this antioxidant, which supports skin and immune health.
- **Fortification:** Most store-bought brands add **Calcium** and **Vitamin D**, often providing more than cow’s milk does.

The "Unhealthy" Side (What to watch for)- **Added Sugars:** "Original" or flavored (Vanilla/Chocolate) versions can contain significant amounts of cane sugar. Always look for the **"Unsweetened"** label.
- **Low Protein:** Almond milk is mostly water. It usually contains only **1g of protein** per cup, compared to **8g** in cow or soy milk. It is not a nutritional substitute for milk in a child's diet.+2
- **Additives:** Some brands use thickeners like **carrageenan** or **gums** (guar, xanthan). While generally recognized as safe, some people find they cause bloating or digestive distress.+1
- **Antinutrients:** Almonds contain phytic acid, which can slightly interfere with the absorption of minerals like calcium, magnesium, and zinc.
