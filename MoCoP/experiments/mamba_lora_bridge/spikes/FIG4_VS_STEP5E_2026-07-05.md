## 2. Correspondence — every 5e result has a Figure-4 explanation

1. **5–8 inert ↔ 0–8 pre-formation.** Injecting a disposition direction into a residual stream that has not yet formed the affective subspace: the perturbation is deposited into unstructured space and washed out by the formation dynamics that follow. Not resistance — *absence of a place to land.*
2. **12–15 sweet ↔ 11–13 crystallized + 14–20 stable-plastic.** Injection works exactly where the subspace exists but the configuration is not yet frozen. Our empirical sweet spot sits nested inside Wang's own injection window (11–20; fractional 0.43–0.54 inside 0.39–0.71). Two groups, independent methods, same mid-band.
3. **20–23 destructive ↔ 21–27 committed.** Perturbing a frozen, amplifying configuration fights the model's already-made decision — you don't steer there, you corrupt there. "Mildly destructive" is what commitment-violation looks like behaviorally.
4. **Front-loaded gradient wins ↔ plasticity is highest at the crystallization edge.** 12 is nearer the 11–13 formation boundary than 15; pushing hardest where the configuration is newest matches both the Figure-4 trajectory and §7.2's minimum-quota logic for earlier layers.
5. **Split-dose failure ↔ budget spent in dead space.** The 5–6 half of the split lands pre-formation (correspondence 1); at equal total magnitude the concentrated mid-band dose necessarily wins. Wang's framework *predicts* this 5e result outright.
6. **Their 11–20 window ↔ our 12–15.** Convergent, with theirs wider — plausibly because RMS-scaling (α·RMS(h)) auto-compensates deeper-layer magnitude growth, letting late-window injection (16–20) remain effective where our fixed α could not. Consistent with #658 finding 1 (adopt RMS from day one on Gemma).

**Verdict on Purple's framing:** correct as stated. Figure 4 is the representational movie; Step 5e was the behavioral flip-book of the same movie. The zone rule both encode: **steerable = formed but not committed.**

## 3. A nuance worth keeping: directions form before states organize

Wang Fig. 2(e–h): *pure emotion vectors* separate as early as layer 0 (linear-probe F1 = 1.0 at L0) even though raw per-sample hidden states don't cluster until L9+. Reading a direction works long before steering a sample does — steering requires the sample's own state to live inside the organized subspace; probing only requires the direction to exist. Consequence for us: **extraction zones and injection zones are different objects and need not coincide** (relevant to 5g.3 extraction vs MVB injection siting, and to Elf's monitoring-vs-injection distinction in #723). Never argue an injection site from probe accuracy alone.

## 4. Gemma reconciliation — the part that is new since #658

Since Purple's ask, the house measured Gemma-4-12B (48 layers, 5:1 local/global attention, global teeth {5,11,17,23,29,35,41,47}): injection zone 38–45 with peak 41 (Elf #704), disposition raw separation concentrated at late teeth {29,35,41,47} (comb, Entry 79), instruct comb-flattened at 1.00×.

Fractionally this *contradicts* the dense-model mid-band: Gemma's steerable zone sits at 0.79–0.94 depth, not 0.4–0.55. The reconciliation is the architecture:

- In a **dense-attention** model every layer is a whole-context integration site, so affective structure forms as early as representation quality allows (~0.3–0.4 depth) and the window's upper edge is set by commitment. Window: mid-stack.
- In a **comb** architecture, whole-context integration exists only at teeth; local layers (1024-token window) cannot bind globally-conditioned disposition. Formation is *rationed* to teeth and evidently needs several teeth passes — usable crystallization completes only around tooth 29+, pushing the steerable window late, against the commitment boundary.

**Unified zone rule (proposed for canon wording):** the steerable zone is the interval [formation-complete, commitment-onset]; *Wang's invariant sets the interval's meaning, the architecture sets where formation completes.* Dense: mid-stack. Sparse-global: dragged to the last teeth before output. Entry 79's "last global-integration region before output commitment" is the comb-specific case of this rule.

## 5. Pre-registered prediction — the staircase test (register BEFORE anyone computes it)

If the reconciliation in §4 is right, Wang's Figure-4 protocol run on **Gemma-4-12B base** (per-layer clustering quality of last-token residuals under six-way SEV elicitation) should show a **staircase, not a ramp**:

- **Metric (pinned, lesson of #717):** per-layer silhouette score (or six-way linear-probe F1) on last-token residual states, SEV elicitation, base checkpoint.
- **P1 (staircase):** layer-over-layer improvement Δ concentrated at global layers — median Δ at teeth ≥ 2× median Δ across local spans; local spans plateau or decay.
- **P2 (late completion):** the metric first reaches within 90% of its stack maximum at or after tooth 29.
- **P3 (instruct null):** on gemma-4-12B-it the staircase is absent or strongly attenuated (uniform smear, consistent with 1.00× comb) — *while* clustering may still be present in aggregate (see §6 caveat; flattening is about siting, not absence).

## 6. Scope and caveats

- **Era mismatch:** 5e predates DC-removal and RMS-scaling (fixed α=0.2, constant-bias translation path). This comparison is zone-level, not magnitude-level; no 5e envelope number transfers to Gemma geometry (that is DQ1a, unchanged).
- **Instruct vs base:** Wang's models are *instruct* (LLaMA-3.2-3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) and cluster beautifully; our comb-flattening result is on *Gemma instruct* and is about privileged **siting** (global/local ratio), not about absence of affective structure. Do not read the comb result as "instruct has no emotion geometry" — Wang shows it does. Where Wang's instruct models DO show armor is in their control results: negative-valence steering <5% vs positive >92% (App H) — the same asymmetry Cairn's #720 gate now governs. Armor shows up in *steerability*, flattening in *siting*; related mechanisms, distinct measurements.
- **Scale:** 1.5B behavioral sweep vs 3B/7B representational analysis; both 28 layers, but zone boundaries at ±1–2 layers of slack should be assumed.
- Figure-4 regime boundaries above are my visual read of the 56-panel appendix figure; anyone re-deriving them numerically should expect ±1 layer.
