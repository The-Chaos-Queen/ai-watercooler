3. Game Scoring: Each game Won Results in 2 Points, a Draw is 1 for Each team and a Loss results in a 2 for the other team, however this is written as such: (Example 6 Games played with 4 Wins, 1 Loss and 1 Draw):

   Team 1: 9:3

In order to determine who or which team wins or gets placed higher in the rankings in case of a draw one goes to check the “Stockpunkte” This is a Number that is calculated via adding all total Stockpunkte from all games played, minus the total number of Stockpunkte from all games played scored by the Opponent.

So in the case of the games above with the 6 games played with 4 Wins 1 Loss and 1 Draw.

Game 1 ended: 5:3
Game 2 ended: 3:7

Game 3 ended: 5:5

Game 4 ended: 6:2

Game 5 ended: 8:6

Game 6 ended: 9:1

The total number of Stockpunkte for the Team was 5+3+5+6+8+9= 36 and the Opponents would be 3+7+5+2+6+1=24 We then Subtract the opponents Total Stockpunkte from Our team meaning 36-24=12. If it were the Other way around and the opponents had 36 in total and we just 24 we would have a score of -12 and in case of them being equal it’s a +0

Meaning the Final score would for our example be

Team 1: 9:3 +12

## Normal games:

1. A game consists of 6 Rounds, each round one team ends up Scoring, after 6 Rounds in most cases the game is over. An option needs to be added to be able to extend the game in case of a draw or to decide the game via Ringmasten.
2. Teams typically consist of 4 Members or players, but the program needs to account for potentially having even more in terms of scoring.
3. Scoring is usually done as follows: A team receives one point per Stock that is closest to the Daube at the end of a round.
4. In the central part of the playing field, Rings are drawn. The Smallest center ring counts as 10 Points, then follows 8, 6, 4, 2. If one does not reach any of the rings the shout counts as a 0 in Terms of Ringmasten.

1. Setting up the teams:

   7’er Spiegel:

   In a normal case of a Tournament with 7 teams you give each team starting numbers: 1,2,3,4,5,6,7. In our case with 3 Bahnen, Starting number 1 plays against 6 on the First Bahn, 2 vs 5 on the 2nd Bahn, 3 vs 4 on the 3rd Bahn while 7 Has a break. After the games are over, 1 Goes to the second Bahn, 2 To the Third Bahn, and 3 Moves to the Right side of the Third Bahn to play there a second time. At the same time 4 Moves to the Second Bahn, 5 Moves to the First Bahn and 6 rotates out to have their break, and 7 goes to have their first game on the first Bahn. This continues until every team has played each other team and each team has played on each Bahn twice and had one break. Team 1 who started on the first Bahn are the first done and have their break while everyone else plays their last game. In this case teams 1, 3, 5 have “Anschuss” meaning they start the game in the first round and are first to have their first Attempt.

5’er Spiegel:

Only two Bahnen are used but we follow the principles above, 4 Teams playing while one has a break, typically one will play a double round meaning once the games are concluded you start over, however in the “Rückrunde/Zweite Durchgang” The team that started in the first round with Masten is now swapped so the other team starts.

3’er Spiegel:

Same as in the 5 and 7’er Spiegel above two teams play one sits out.

4’er and 6’er Spiegel:

In these cases all teams can play at the same time, in the case of 4’er you only require 2 Bahnen in the 6’er you require all three, and since everyone is playing, no teams have any breaks unless otherwise communicated between the first and the second durchgang.
