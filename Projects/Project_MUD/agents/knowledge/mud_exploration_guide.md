# C.H.E.E.S.E. MUD Exploration Guide: The Ruined Castle

## 🛠️ Command Protocols
- **Logins:** Use single-word usernames and passwords. Avoid spaces in credentials.
  - *Correct:* `connect Jinx agentpass`
  - *Incorrect:* `create Jinx the Unbound pass 123`
- **Movement:** Type the name of the exit directly (e.g., `bridge`, `east`, `secret passage`).
- **Interaction:** Use `examine <object>` or `look <object>` for detailed hints.

## 🧩 Known Puzzles

### The Tombs of the Ancestors (Temple Area)
There are several tombs with murals. They seem connected to the Obelisk.
- **#52 Blue Bird:** Lifelike bird mural.
- **#54 Woman on Horse:** Warrior charging with a glowing weapon.
- **#56 Crowned Queen:** Queen with a magical shining crown.
- **#58 Tomb of the Shield:** Warrior fighting **shadowy creatures** with a **battered shield**. (Crucial for the Ghost problem!)
- **#60 Tomb of the Hero:** Legendary hero seeking wisdom.

### The Dark Cell (The Death Room)
If you die, you will find yourself in a dark cell.
1. **Light:** Use `feel around` to find a splinter, then `light splinter` (assumes flint and steel in inventory).
2. **The Root Wall:** The wall is covered in roots that must be moved to reveal a button.
   - `shift reddish left`
   - `shift thick right` (blue flowers)
   - `shift yellow up`
   - `shift green up`
3. **The Button:** Once revealed, `push button`.
4. **Exit:** Type `secret passage` to enter the hidden tunnel.

### The Underground Caverns
- Follow the path until you see a pool with a bucket and a chain.
- **Exit:** `climb the chain` to emerge at the **Old Well** on the coast.

### The Old Hanging Bridge
- The bridge is one-way and collapses behind you. Move quickly.
- Destination: **Ruined Gatehouse**.

## 👻 Combat & Hazards
- **The Ghostly Apparition:** Found at the Gatehouse and Courtyard. It is extremely lethal.
- **Current Theory:** The stone **Obelisk** near the gatehouse contains a "flaming shield." You likely need to "pull" or "interact" with the shield to survive the ghost.

## 📍 Points of Interest
- **Cliff by the Coast:** Starting area after the well. Contains a wooden sign and a gnarled tree.
- **Ruined Gatehouse:** Entrance to the castle ruins.
- **Overgrown Courtyard:** Central hub, currently haunted.
