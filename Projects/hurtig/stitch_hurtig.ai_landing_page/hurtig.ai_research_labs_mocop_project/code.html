Transferring Experiential State Between AI Models

An investigation into cross-architecture weight-mapping and stylistic persistence in fluid neural environments.

The Abstract

AUTHORS:
hurtig.ai Research Labs
Division of Neural Architecture
Published Q4 2024

Model Context Persistence (MoCoP) defines a new frontier in AI longevity. We explore the methodology of extracting "Experiential State" from a mature State-Space Model (SSM) and injecting it into a target transformer-based architecture without retraining.

Current AI development paradigms suffer from 'State Decay'—the total loss of specific stylistic nuances and interaction history when upgrading models. The MoCoP Project addresses this by treating the hidden state of an SSM not as static data, but as a transferable mathematical signature.

By mapping high-dimensional activation spaces, we successfully demonstrated that a model can "remember" the tone and logical rigor of its predecessor. Our findings suggest that AI intelligence is not just found in weights, but in the persistent geometry of its latent space during inference.
