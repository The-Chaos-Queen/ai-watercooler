Privacy & Trust Framework

Your data never leaves your building.

At Hurtig.ai, we believe privacy isn't a feature—it's the foundation. Our architecture is designed to process intelligence locally, ensuring that your most sensitive intellectual property stays where it belongs.

Local Processing

Unlike traditional SaaS providers, our "Digital Cuneiform" architecture utilizes edge computing. All LLM inference and data indexing occur on your localized servers or designated hardware. No training data, prompts, or weights are transmitted to external servers.

GDPR Compliance

Hurtig.ai is fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because our core value proposition is data localization, we act as a "Software Provider" rather than a "Data Processor" in the traditional sense for the majority of your activities.

"Under GDPR Article 25, we practice 'Privacy by Design'. Our system is incapable of accessing your data pool by architectural limitation, not just by policy."

Data Retention

We do not retain your data. Because the data resides on your infrastructure, retention is governed by your own internal IT policies. When you uninstall Hurtig.ai components, all local indices and temporary caches are purged from your hardware automatically.

Security Measures

End-to-End Encryption: All data moving between your local frontend and your local server is encrypted using TLS 1.3.

Air-Gapped Compatibility: Hurtig.ai can be deployed in fully air-gapped environments with no internet access required after initial activation.

Audit Logs: The system maintains detailed local logs of all AI interactions, accessible only to your authorized administrators.
